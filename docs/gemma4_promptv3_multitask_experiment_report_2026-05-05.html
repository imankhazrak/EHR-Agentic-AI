  • Lipid next-visit performance is best in this snapshot with zero_shot_plus (ACC 79.13, F1 63.56).

  • Diabetes, hypertension, and obesity tasks are strong across approaches, especially in few-shot.
  • Stroke remains difficult for all approaches (low precision/recall despite high specificity), consistent with class imbalance.
